## <i class="ph ph-pulse"></i> Active
- **Thundermail: Webmail** What would an email service be without webmail? We will spend time making the proof of concept more usable, and enable this as an experimental feature for users willing to try new things early. The scope will initially be limited to basic folder visibility and message display. An alpha version has shipped to users, and we are continuing to work on the initial milestones, including integrating webmail into the main product headers. Work on webmail will continue into 2027.
- **Account Offboarding** Work has begun on the account offboarding process. This is a complex project with many moving pieces, so we are breaking it down to focus on the highest-impact parts first. Work will continue through Q4 and into early 2027.
- **Accessibility Improvements** An ongoing effort across all our products. We are currently running a UX audit and usability study on Thundermail webmail.

## <i class="ph ph-calendar-blank"></i> Planned
- **Import email, contacts, and calendar feature** As one of the top ideas on ideas.tb.pro, we have added this suggestion from: https://ideas.tb.pro/p/import-email-calendar-and-contacts-feature and it fits into our quest to make Thundermail the users’ primary inbox. Now planned for 2027.


##  <i class="ph ph-rocket-launch"></i> Shipped
- **Improve Thundermail Add-on Integration** The add-on that enables Thundermail features in Thunderbird desktop now ships as a system add-on, keeping the barrier to entry low and making these features easier to discover and use within Thunderbird. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **Active Sessions** Give the user a view on their active sessions and allow them to end the session. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **Observability and Alerting** Improved monitoring and alerting across our services. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **Services Logging** Centralized logging for our services. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **ArgoCD and CI/CD Improvements** Improved our deployment pipelines and continuous delivery tooling. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **Staff OIDC Instance** Set up a staff OpenID Connect (OIDC) instance for MZLA services. <div class="date-badge">September 2026</div>
- **MFA Support** Multifactor authentication via TOTP or a similar method is an essential security feature. <div class="date-badge">June 2026</div>
- **Send UI Completion** Send was shipped as a system add-on and that UI work was completed. <div class="date-badge">June 2026</div>
- **Thundermail: Spam Filtering** The spam filtering was very aggressive, causing many emails to unintentionally land in the spam folder. The filtering mechanism has been changed to significantly improve this behavior. A big thank you to Mauro and team from Stalwart, who have made the spam detection algorithm more sophisticated and accurate! <div class="date-badge">March 2026</div>
